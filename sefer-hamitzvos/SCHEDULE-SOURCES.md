Schedule: https://github.com/hebcal/hebcal-learning/blob/main/src/seferHaMitzvot.json
Cycle arithmetic: https://github.com/hebcal/hebcal-learning/blob/main/src/seferHaMitzvotBase.ts
Cross-check: Chabad cycle 46, https://w2.chabad.org/media/pdf/526/eWnk5263924.pdf

Cycle repeats every 339 days, starting April 29, 1984. Four introductory days have no picture cards. Repeated mitzvos retain the same cards. The 613 original cards follow first appearance in the study cycle; matching checked for cards 1, 475, 476 and 477. The complete numbering is derived from that ordering, not independently reviewed image by image. Days 140, 161, 258 and 259 follow the current Chabad table (some older editions differ). Days 258 and 259 use N295 and N296 respectively, matching Chabad's primary 2026-10-18/19 schedule.

Hebrew dates use the browser's Hebrew calendar implementation. The classroom date follows Kingston, PA (America/New_York), advancing at computed sunset; it does not request a student's location. Sunset calculation: SunCalc https://github.com/mourner/suncalc, license included. Date navigation remains local to this activity.

Cartoon artwork replacements: 475–481. Remaining pages retain the original outlines until individually redesigned. Coloring is local to the student browser and existing class access controls remain authoritative.

Week of October 12, 2026: 3 Cheshvan / Oct 14 P194 (478); 4 Cheshvan / Oct 15 N269 (479) and P204 (480); 5–6 Cheshvan / Oct 16–17 P236 (481). New artwork follows the existing approved cartoon style.
