Give a teacher Shorashim words

Each teacher has their own Shorashim library under their own account. New teachers start with an empty library. Choose a teacher, then tick which of your lists to copy into their library. It is a copy: your own classes are not changed, and the teacher decides which of their classes get the words.

Students

Each student belongs to one school, and only you can choose it. Then tick the classes they are in — only that school’s classes are shown. Ticking adds the student to the class; unticking takes them out (their work is kept). Names, passcodes and sign-in don’t change.

📋 Bulk Upload

Upload a CSV to add classes, teachers, principals and students. Each person is on one row. Put every class a student or teacher is in into the Classes column, separated by semicolons, for example B2 ET Morning; B2 ET Afternoon. A student with five classes is still one row, one passcode and one account. Download template CSV

Columns: Type (Class, Principal, Teacher, Student), Name, Email (teachers and principals), Passcode (students, exactly 6 digits), Classes, and optional Student ID (your own roster number, used to recognise the student next time). Classes first, then teachers and principals, then students. Older files with one row per class still work: rows with the same name and passcode become one student.

↩️ Undo CSV Import

Takes back a CSV import that went wrong. You choose the import, see every account and class it touches, download a backup, and confirm once. Only accounts the import itself made, and that are still empty, are removed. Students who were on the site before, anything with saved work, classes and teachers are never deleted, and everything removed can be put back.
