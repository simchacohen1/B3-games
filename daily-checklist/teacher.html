Sync to Mashpia: one-time setup (about 20 seconds, once per computer)

1. Show your bookmarks bar (Chrome: press Ctrl+Shift+B).
2. Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar: B3 Mashpia Sync
3. Press Sync to Mashpia above. Mashpia opens in a new tab. Log in to Mashpia there if it asks, then click the B3 Mashpia Sync bookmark once on that tab. The sync runs by itself.

After that, just press Sync to Mashpia here each time. Keep that Mashpia tab open; if you close it, repeat step 3. Your Mashpia password is never stored or sent to us.
