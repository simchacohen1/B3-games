🎤 Rashi Letters Recordings

Listen and follow along. All letters count as correct: tap a letter that was not recognized (tap again to undo). The text is split into 5 equal parts and each part with no missed letter earns 1 point (perfect = 5). The student paid 2 points to send it; if it earned less than 5 he can record it again (and pay 2 again), and you will see the new one here. Delete removes a bad recording and refunds the 2 points. There is no Save button: it saves a few seconds after your last tap, and you can change it again at any time (points are adjusted for you, never paid twice). If every letter was right, press Done.
