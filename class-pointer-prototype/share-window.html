🖥 Sharing Controls

Keep this window open while working in your learning activities.

When your teacher enables sharing, click Share My Screen below. Your browser asks permission. Choose your activity tab, a window, or the entire screen.

Sharing a tab follows that tab as you change activities. Sharing a window or screen also shows changes between tabs.

Click Stop Sharing below, use your browser’s stop control, or close this window to end sharing.

No video is saved. If no button appears, sign in to your class and ask your teacher to enable Student Screens.
