Modeh Ani — Chabad Reading Demo

Version 2 · microphone recording, playback, and experimental speech comparison

This is a working recording and comparison prototype, not a validated AI pronunciation grader. It does not award points or change Fun Torah Tools. Browser speech recognition can mishear Chabad words or fail entirely; a matching transcript does not prove the nekudos were correct. Test with an adult first, not identifiable student audio.

1. Read Modeh Ani

The Hebrew is for the reader; the Chabad transliteration is the reference used by the text comparison.

2. Record and hear yourself

Ready. Press Start recording and allow microphone permission.

Recording and playback operate independently of speech recognition. No audio is uploaded by this page. Browser speech-recognition services may process audio separately if available.

3. Experimental comparison

After recording, Chrome or Edge may provide a speech transcript. Because Chabad pronunciation is not a built-in speech-recognition language, you can correct the transcript below before comparison.

This is a text comparison only. You must listen to the recording to assess pronunciation.
The actual phonetic sound of a word cannot be reconstructed reliably from an ordinary Hebrew transcript without nekudos. This version intentionally never labels a microphone recording as “correct” based on speech recognition alone. This standalone site has no Firebase integration, no student login and no student awards.
