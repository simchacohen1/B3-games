Chabad Reading Lab

Modeh Ani • Experimental standalone demonstration • No accounts or points
Important: This demo compares a browser-generated speech transcript with Chabad English transliteration, not raw audio phonemes. It is not validated for nekudos or student grades. Browsers may misrecognize Ashkenazic Hebrew. Browser speech recognition may use an external provider, but this page does not upload or save a recording to Fun Torah Tools.

1. Read the Hebrew aloud

מוֹדֶה אֲנִי לְפָנֶיךָ מֶלֶךְ חַי וְקַיָּם שֶׁהֶחֱזַרְתָּ בִּי נִשְׁמָתִי בְּחֶמְלָה רַבָּה אֱמוּנָתֶךָ

Moideh ani lefonecho, Melech chai v'kayom, Shehechezarto bi nishmosi, b'chemloh, Rabboh emunosecho.

Expected sounds follow the classroom Chabad/Ashkenazic transliteration. Vowels are intentionally preserved.
